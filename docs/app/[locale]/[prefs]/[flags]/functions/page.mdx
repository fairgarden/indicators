# Functions

## `@fairgarden/indicators`

For `lib/indicators.ts`:

- [`createIndicators`](/functions/createIndicators) — declare the locales, preferences and flags

On the object `createIndicators` returns, for layouts and pages:

- [`generateStaticParams`](/functions/generateStaticParams) — the variants to prerender, all at once or per level
- [`read`](/functions/read) — the locale, preferences and flags a route's params name
- [`layout` and `page`](/functions/layout) — the same, as a wrapper
- [`encode` and `decode`](/functions/encode) — a segment from values, and back
- [`href`, `splitLocale`, `negotiate`](/functions/href) — locale-aware paths, server side
- `stylesheets()` and the `Stylesheets` component — the links for indicators expressed through a [stylesheet](/overview/stylesheets): the global ones in the layout, and with `only`, the ones a page links itself

Standalone, for tests and tooling:

- [Helpers](/functions/helpers) — `negotiateLocale`, `localizeHref`, `mountHref`, `indicatorsRewrites` and friends

## `@fairgarden/indicators/withFairGardenIndicators`

For `next.config.ts`:

- [`withFairGardenIndicators`](/functions/withFairGardenIndicators) — add the rewrites, redirects and headers
- [`detectExclusions`](/functions/helpers) — what a look at the app keeps out of the locale rewrite

## `@fairgarden/indicators/link`

For a client module such as `lib/link.ts`:

- [`createNavigation`](/functions/createNavigation) — `Link`, `useHref`, `useIndicators`, `useLocale` and `usePref`, bound to the app's config

## `@fairgarden/indicators/proxy`

For `proxy.ts`:

- [`createLocaleProxy`](/functions/createLocaleProxy) — language negotiation at the site root
